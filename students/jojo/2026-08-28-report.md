- **fnd-L3-01** · Foundations & economics · Series 7 core · incorrect · 20s
  - A FINRA member broker-dealer violates an MSRB rule while selling municipal bonds. Which body is responsible for enforcing that rule against the firm?
  - Key: FINRA
  - Chose: The state securities administrator
  - Why: The MSRB writes rules for municipal dealers but has no enforcement arm; FINRA enforces MSRB rules against broker-dealers (bank regulators do so for bank dealers). The MSRB cannot regulate issuers at all (Tower Amendment). (02-foundations.md §3.D.3) *(M6)*
- **opt-L2-05** · Options · SIE level · incorrect · 33s
  - A registered representative is opening an options account for a new customer. The Options Disclosure Document (ODD) must be furnished to the customer:
  - Key: at or before the time the account is approved for options trading
  - Chose: no later than the settlement date of the customer's first options trade
  - Why: Under FINRA Rule 2360 the ODD is delivered at or prior to account approval, before any trade. The 15-day clock applies to returning the signed options agreement, not the ODD (06-options.md §3.15). *(M22)*
- **reg-L3-01** · Laws, underwriting, trading · Series 7 core · incorrect · 52s
  - A broker-dealer sells shares in the aftermarket of an initial public offering of a company whose stock will be listed on Nasdaq. For how long after the effective date must the dealer deliver a prospectus to aftermarket purchasers?
  - Key: 25 days
  - Chose: No delivery requirement applies once the offering is effective
  - Why: Aftermarket delivery under Rule 174 / Rule 15c2-8 is 90 days for a non-listed IPO, 40 days for a non-listed follow-on, 25 days for an IPO that will be exchange- or Nasdaq-listed, and 0 days for a listed follow-on. *(M37)*
- **debt-L4-04** · Debt securities · Series 7 hard · incorrect · 174s
  - An investor buys a $1,000 Treasury Inflation-Protected Security with a 3% coupon. Over the first year the CPI rises 4%. Approximately how much interest will the investor receive for that year?
  - Key: $31.20
  - Chose: $30.00
  - Why: TIPS keep the coupon rate fixed and adjust the principal for CPI: $1,000 x 1.04 = $1,040 adjusted principal, and 3% of $1,040 = $31.20 (04-debt.md §3.9.4). The inflation adjustment raises principal (taxed annually as phantom income), not the coupon rate. *(M25)*
- **muni-L3-01** · Municipal securities · Series 7 core · incorrect · 45s
  - A customer in the 28% federal marginal tax bracket is considering a municipal bond yielding 3.60%. What is the bond's taxable-equivalent yield?
  - Key: 5.00%
  - Chose: 4.61%
  - Why: TEY = tax-free yield ÷ (1 − marginal rate) = 3.60% ÷ 0.72 = 5.00% (05-municipal.md §3.3). Multiplying by 0.72 to get 2.59% is the tax-free-equivalent formula run in the wrong direction. *(M26)*
- **pkg-L4-02** · Funds, annuities, retirement, tax · Series 7 hard · incorrect · 13s
  - An investor buys $600 of a mutual fund on the first of each month for four months at prices of $12, $10, $8, and $10 per share. The investor's average cost per share is closest to:
  - Key: $9.80
  - Chose: $10.00
  - Why: Shares bought: 600/12 = 50, 600/10 = 60, 600/8 = 75, 600/10 = 60, total 245 shares for $2,400; average cost = $2,400 / 245 = $9.80. The average price, (12 + 10 + 8 + 10) / 4 = $10.00, is higher, which is the DCA effect. See §3.2 (Dollar-cost averaging). *(M29)*
- **pkg-L4-04** · Funds, annuities, retirement, tax · Series 7 hard · incorrect · 44s
  - A variable annuity is annuitized with an assumed interest rate (AIR) of 4%. The separate account earns 8% in month 1, 5% in month 2, and 3% in month 3. The third payment, which reflects the month 2 return, compared with the second payment will be:
  - Key: higher, because 5% exceeds the 4% AIR
  - Chose: lower, because 5% is less than the 8% earned the previous month
  - Why: Each payment is compared with the AIR, never with the prior period's actual return: 5% > 4% AIR, so the third payment rises even though 5% < 8%. (The 3% month 3 return, below the AIR, would make the fourth payment fall.) See §3.5 (Annuitization and the AIR). *(M30)*
- **debt-L4-03** · Debt securities · Series 7 hard · incorrect · 51s
  - A 6% bond with 10 years to maturity is purchased at $1,100. It is callable in 5 years at 103. Using the exam approximation method, the yield to call is closest to:
  - Key: 4.32%
  - Chose: 6.00%
  - Why: YTC = [annual interest + (call price - purchase price) / years to call] / [(call price + purchase price) / 2] = [$60 + ($1,030 - $1,100)/5] / [($1,030 + $1,100)/2] = $46 / $1,065 = 4.32% (04-debt.md §3.5.4). 4.76% is the approximate YTM and 5.45% the current yield; on a premium bond YTC is the lowest. *(M23)*
